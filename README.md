### About

Technical founder. Systems architect. Product engineer.

I lead engineering at **Skipp**  a quick‑commerce platform delivering branded fashion in 30–90 minutes across Indian metros. My work sits at the intersection of **real‑time logistics**, **AI‑driven inventory**, and **high‑performance mobile commerce**. I design and operate the full stack, from cloud infrastructure to React Native experience, always optimising for execution speed and system reliability.

- CTO & operator – scaling teams, systems, and delivery networks
- Systems thinker – event‑driven architectures, monorepos, API design
- Product‑focused engineer – shipping features that move metrics
- Builder – fashion‑tech, quick‑commerce, AI logistics

---

### What I’m Building

**Skipp** – hyperlocal fashion, delivered in minutes.

- React Native consumer & rider apps with Expo
- Real‑time inventory and order orchestration engine
- AI‑powered logistics: demand forecasting, route optimisation, dynamic slotting
- Cloud‑native backend (Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis) on AWS
- Delivery‑partner management and live tracking
- High‑frequency commerce systems that handle flash sales and surge loads

Every piece is designed for speed, scale, and a seamless customer experience.
